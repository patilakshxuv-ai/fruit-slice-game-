About Fruit Slice

Fruit Slice is a free browser arcade game operated by Akshay Patil. It offers Classic and Zen modes for players who want a quick fruit-slicing challenge. Swipe with a mouse or touchscreen to slice fruit, build combos, and avoid bombs in Classic mode.

The game is designed for entertainment and can be played in a modern web browser. No account is required to access the game.
